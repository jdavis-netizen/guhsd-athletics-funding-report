Coaching Paid Through ASB Trust Accounts
GUHSD Athletics Data Brief Granite Hills Athletics
April 2026
Granite Hills paid $96,009 from student trust accounts to compensate 53 coaches not on district stipend, authorized through the GUHSD "Request To Pay Coach – Trust" Google Form and routed through district HR via PAF. GUHSD allocates 52 stipend slots ($313,200), split across 58 coach positions (e.g., one frosh football stipend split four ways). An additional $27,710 in non-league officials, clearance, and fees was invoiced to ASB, for a FY 2025-26 total of $123,719+.

Summary

  • The 52 district stipends are split across 58 coach positions. Head coaches used the Trust Pay form to authorize 53 additional coach payments totaling $96,009 from booster trusts, routed through district HR via PAF and funded by ASB.
  • Trust coach pay ($96,009) + non-league officials/clearance/etc. ($27,710) + the $35,393 officials difference = ~$159,000+ paid through ASB/boosters at Granite Hills in FY 2025-26. Scaled district-wide (estimate): $1.0M–$1.4M+.
  • Granite Hills' $20K AD Athletic Needs Fund (#5032-06) is exhausted — $26,391 committed by 4/29 against $20K budget; covered routine equipment, tournament fees, and 3 GUHSD "Athletic Needs" invoices.
