An April 2026 American Enterprise Institute report (n=262,600, Indiana) finds varsity athletes are 12.9 percentage points less likely to be chronically absent than non-athletes (7.7 pp after demographic controls), with an effect ~2× larger for low-income students. "It is almost certain that athletes' improved attendance during their sports seasons is predominantly caused by their active sports participation." — Malkus and Hollon, An Extra Point for Attendance: The Impact of High School Varsity Athletics on Absenteeism, American Enterprise Institute, April 8, 2026 (n=262,600 Indiana HS students; day-level Indiana DOE attendance × IHSAA varsity rosters; student fixed-effects causal model).

Athletics participation is rising — even as enrollment declines

  • CA HS enrollment has declined 8 consecutive years (~1.3%/yr). CIF participation: 763,867 → 806,979 → 852,575 (2024-25 all-time high; +11.6% two-year).
  • Athletics participation grew 11.6% over two years while California high school enrollment declined.
