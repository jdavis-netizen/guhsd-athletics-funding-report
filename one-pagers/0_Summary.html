The seven findings

#TopicKey MetricOne-line finding
1 Athletic Trainers $35 / hr GUHSD pays its AT a $22,500 stipend (~$35/hr); CATA recommends $67–$73/hr for SoCal Region 5–6. Six of seven verified SD County peers staff AT as full-time classified. See also Pierpoint 2018 (6× / 3× recurrent injury rate ratios) and Morgan Hill OCR 2023 (Title IX).
2 Coaching Stipends $1,190–$1,640 difference Helix Charter (in the Grossmont League) pays head varsity football $1,190–$1,640 more than GUHSD at every experience tier (2025-26 schedule). Sweetwater pays 40% more at entry. AD comparison: GUHSD second-lowest in the SD County peer set at $6,227 top tier vs. Helix $13,675 / Steele Canyon $13,434. ★ Helix is ranked #14 in California (USA Today HSS / Niche, Apr 29 2026) — no GUHSD comprehensive site appears in the top 25.
3 Attendance & LCFF +$1.7M / pp ADA AEI April 2026 report (n=262,600): athletes 12.9 pp less likely to be chronically absent; effect 2× larger for low-income students. 1 pp attendance ≈ $1.7M+ in CA LCFF revenue.
4 Walk-On Coaches 52-slot cap GUHSD caps stipends at 52 per school across 24+ sports; positions above the cap are unpaid or booster-funded. LAUSD reported 58% walk-on coaches (2013–14). At Granite Hills, 52 stipends are split across 58 coaches.
5 Officials & Ref Fees 38% district share GUHSD allocates $22,000/school. Granite Hills FY 2025-26 verified competition overhead: $57,393. District covers 38%; ASB / boosters cover the remaining $35,393. CHSSO 2025-26 fee schedule verified for every major sport.
6 Coaching Paid via ASB $96,009 Granite Hills paid $96,009 from trust accounts to compensate 53 coaches not on district stipend, authorized through the GUHSD "Request To Pay Coach – Trust" Google Form and routed through district HR via PAF. + $27,710 non-league officials/clearance/etc. = $123,719+ paid through ASB at one site.
7 The Research Case 99.4% grad rate NFHS-compiled research + AEI 2026: athletes graduate at 99.4% vs. 93.5%, carry +0.81 GPA, and miss ~half as many school days; differences are largest for low-income students. NFHS national norm for activities funding: 1–3% of budget.
